# Calculation Methodology

The calculators available on the project are designed to provide educational retirement planning estimates.

## Variables

- Monthly Contribution
- Annual Return
- Investment Period
- Retirement Age
- Expected Inflation
- Annuity Percentage
- Pension Withdrawal

## Formula

Future Value = Monthly Investment × Compounding Growth

Additional calculations include:

- Estimated retirement corpus
- Estimated monthly pension
- Total contributions
- Total returns
- Tax saving estimates

Results are illustrative and should not be considered financial advice.
